# Plan — Adapting/Infusing Object-Oriented Ontology (OOO) in Literature and Poetry

## Research question

How has object-oriented ontology (OOO) — and the wider speculative-realist
family it belongs to — been adapted, translated, or "infused" into literary
theory, literary criticism, and poetics/poetry-making? What vocabularies,
methods, and case studies do practitioners in literary studies actually use
when they carry OOO's terms (flat ontology, withdrawal, sensual vs. real
objects, allure, hyperobjects, alien phenomenology, tool-being, vicarious
causation) into reading and writing poems and fiction?

## Scope

- Core OOO theorists as primary anchors: Graham Harman (tool-being, weird
  realism, allure), Timothy Morton (hyperobjects, ecopoetics, dark ecology),
  Levi Bryant (onticology, democracy of objects), Ian Bogost (alien
  phenomenology, carpentry).
- Literary-side uptake: ecocriticism/ecopoetics, "thing theory" (Bill Brown)
  as an adjacent/rival lineage, new materialism, weird fiction criticism
  (Lovecraft-as-Harman's case study), speculative realism and the novel,
  OOO-inflected poetics and creative-writing practice.
- Persian-language reception (if any) per protocol — check for translations
  or discussions of OOO in Persian literary/philosophical journals.
- Excluded: OOO's internal metaphysical disputes not tied to literary
  application; general environmental humanities not touching OOO/objects.
